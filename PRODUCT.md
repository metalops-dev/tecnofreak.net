# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Spanish-speaking readers interested in technology: general audiences, enthusiasts, and technical professionals. Content should be useful to all three groups.

## Product Purpose

Inform and explain technology news, products, and trends to help readers understand them.

## Positioning

tecnofreak.net is an independent Spanish-language technology blog covering news, gadget and software analysis, and opinions without noise or unnecessary jargon.

## Operating Context

Readers can explore articles by category and tag, browse the archive, search, and follow updates via RSS. They can also explore recommended products; the site discloses that it earns commissions from Amazon affiliate purchases.

The site has an editorial system based on Payload CMS.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: tecnofreak.net.
- An independent, clear editorial voice without unnecessary jargon.

## Evidence on Hand

- Affiliate disclosure: `src/app/(frontend)/productos/page.tsx`.
- RSS feed: `src/app/(frontend)/rss.xml/route.ts`.

## Product Principles

- Inform and explain rather than chase noise.
- Preserve editorial independence and clarity.
- Serve general readers, enthusiasts, and technical professionals.
- Disclose affiliate relationships where applicable.
